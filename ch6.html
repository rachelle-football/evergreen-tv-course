Teacher: how to prepare the two clips (before class)
  1. Record yourself saying any sentence for 10 seconds on your phone. That is Clip A.
  2. Open the free F5-TTS page on Hugging Face (search "F5-TTS Hugging Face space") on your own computer.
  3. Upload your recording as the reference voice, type a new sentence (for example "Today there is no homework for EB7"), and press Synthesize. Download the result. That is Clip B.
  4. Only use your own voice. Never upload a student's voice to any website.
02

How an AI copies a voice

7 minutes · the lesson

1 · It listened

Voice AIs learned from thousands of hours of people talking, with the words written underneath.

2 · It finds your voice print

From a few seconds of you, it measures how high your voice is, how fast you talk, and the shape of your sounds.

3 · It speaks as you

Then it can say any sentence in your voice, even words you never said.

What would you do?

Our voice rules

Never clone a voice without permission. A voice alone proves nothing. Agree a family safe word.
