05

Inside the AI's brain

Period 2 · 10 minutes · the brain is a team

How does an AI decide if an animal is a 🐱 cat or a 🐶 dog? Inside its brain, little helpers vote.

1Each helper looks at ONE thing

👂 the ears, 📏 the size, or 🔊 the sound. Then it votes: 🐱 or 🐶.

2Stars = how much the AI trusts it

⭐⭐⭐ = its vote counts 3 points. 0 = the AI ignores it.

3The AI adds the points

More 🐱 points → “Cat!” More 🐶 points → “Dog!”

Example · a Chihuahua 🐶 (pointy ears, small, says woof)
👂 Ears: pointy → votes 🐱⭐⭐⭐🐱 +3
📏 Size: small → votes 🐱⭐⭐⭐🐱 +3
🔊 Sound: woof → votes 🐶0 (ignored)🐶 +0

Total: 🐱 6 points, 🐶 0 points → the AI says “Cat!” ✗ Wrong! The sound helper was right, but the AI didn't listen to it.

Your job: fix the AI's brain

1. Press “Test the AI”. 2. Look at its ✗ mistakes. 3. Change the stars, and test again, until the AI gets 8 of 8.

The AI's answers

What did you learn?

The helpers are the AI's neurons. The stars are called weights: how much the AI listens to each one. When an AI learns, it changes the weights by itself, again and again, to make fewer and fewer mistakes. You just did it by hand!

ChatGPT's brain has billions of these weights. That's why it can do so much.
06

Creation Lab: train a REAL AI

Period 2 · 25 minutes · Google Teachable Machine · no code

Now leave the app and train a REAL AI, the same way engineers do: with photos of 3 school objects.

First: how will you give it photos?

To learn what a pencil is, the AI needs to see many photos of pencils (like your drawings in the mission). There are 2 ways to give it photos: film your real objects with a camera, or use ready photos. Press the button to see which one this computer can do.

(the camera does NOT turn on)

📷 With a camera

Put your pencil, notebook and eraser on the table, and film them (never faces).

📁 Without a camera

Use the photo pack: 90 training photos and 8 test photos, made for you.

Open the downloaded file: Windows → right-click → “Extract all”.

🛡️ Lab rules
  • The camera films only objects on the table. Never faces, never people.
  • Your teacher is with you during the lab.
  • Don't press “Save project to Drive”: it saves your photos online.
Example · Teach AI to recognise school objects
✏️Class 1Pencil30 images
📓Class 2Notebook30 images
🧽Class 3Eraser30 images
Total90training images
Step 1 · Collect data
  1. 🧪 Open Teachable Machine Choose Standard image model.
  2. Rename Class 1, Class 2, and press Add a class for the 3rd. Write the names of your 3 objects.
  3. 📷 With a camera: in each class press Webcam, then Hold to Record. Turn the object slowly: different sides, close and far. About 30 images each.
  4. 📁 Without a camera: in each class press Upload, then Choose images from your files, open the right folder (pencil, notebook or eraser) and select all its photos (Ctrl + A).
Step 2 · Train the AI

Press Train Model and wait. Don't change tab while it learns! The AI is looking for the patterns in your photos.

Step 3 · Test the AI

Give it something new it never saw. 📷 Show the camera a friend's pencil. 📁 In Preview, change Webcam to File and drop a test photo (test-1, test-2 or test-3). Look at the bars.

How to read the result

In Teachable Machine, under Preview, look at Output: one bar for each class. The longest bar is the AI's answer, and the % is how sure it is.

✅ A new pencil: the AI is right
Pencil96%
Notebook3%
Eraser1%
😈 An apple: the AI is sure… and wrong!
Pencil6%
Notebook2%
Eraser92%

The AI only knows pencil, notebook and eraser. It has never seen an apple, so it must choose one of the 3, and it says “eraser, 92%”. AI doesn't know what it doesn't know.

Step 4 · Try to trick your AI! 😈

Can you fool it? Try each trick, then say what happened.

Step 5 · Save your first AI model
  1. Take a screenshot of your best test (the bars in Preview).
  2. Optional: Export Model → Download to keep your model.
