Train, then test

Period 1 · 7 minutes · the lesson

Every AI is made in three steps. Engineers use these exact words.

Step 1

Examples

Lots of labelled examples: "this is a pencil", "this is a bottle". Engineers call this training data.

→
Step 2

Training

The computer looks for patterns in the examples. The result is called a model.

→
Step 3

Testing

Give the model new examples it never saw, and count how often it is right. Like a real exam: new questions!

05

When AI is unfair

Period 2 · 10 minutes · try it yourself

This AI learned "tree" from 6 drawings. Look at them in its memory below: every tree is round on top. What happens if you draw a Christmas tree?

Step 1

Draw a pointy tree

Draw a Christmas tree 🎄 (a triangle on a trunk) and press Ask.

Step 2

Watch it fail

It does not say tree! Why? It never saw a tree like yours.

Step 3

Fix it

Draw 3 pointy trees and press Teach: this is a TREE after each one. Then draw a new one and ask again.

This is called bias

When the examples are one-sided, the AI is unfair to everything it did not see.

It happens in real life, and not only with trees. In 2018, researchers tested face programs from big companies: they made under 1% mistakes on light-skinned men, and up to 35% on dark-skinned women, because most of their examples were light-skinned men. (Gender Shades study)
06

Mission: trick the AI

Period 2 · 15 minutes · teach it lies, then fix it

A brand new AI, with an empty brain. You will teach it lies: circles are squares, squares are circles. What will it believe?

What just happened

The AI is not clever or stupid. It believes whatever its examples say. Wrong examples make a wrong AI.

Engineers say: garbage in, garbage out. Real AIs learn from millions of examples written by people. If people made mistakes, the AI learns the mistakes too.
07

Inside the AI's brain

Period 2 · 8 minutes · the brain is a team

An AI's brain is a team of little helpers. Each one gives its opinion. The AI decides who to listen to more.

The job: is it a 🐱 cat or a 🐶 dog?

Your AI has 3 helpers. Choose how much it trusts each one (0 to 3 stars). At first, it only listens to how the animal looks.

The AI's answers

What did you learn?

The helpers are the AI's neurons. The stars are how much it listens to each one. When an AI learns, it changes the stars by itself, again and again, until it makes no mistakes.

ChatGPT has billions of helpers. That's why it can do so much.
