02

The prompt recipe

7 minutes · the lesson

Weak prompt

"Write sports news about our school."

Gemini does not know your school. So it invents team names, scores and players, and says them as if they were true.

Recipe prompt

Role + Task + Context + Format

You give it the facts, the job and the style. It stops guessing.

Build a recipe prompt together. Change any part and watch the prompt change.
