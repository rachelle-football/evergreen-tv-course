01

What makes a story?

6 minutes · the lesson

A design card says what the future looks like. A story makes people feel it. Every good story has the same 4 parts.

1 · Character

Who is it about? We care about a person, not a building.

2 · Problem

What goes wrong, or what do they want?

3 · The future helps

Your design is the hero's secret weapon.

4 · Ending

How did they change? End with a smile or a surprise.
