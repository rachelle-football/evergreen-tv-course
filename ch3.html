One sentence, one website

5 minutes · teacher demo

Watch: the teacher types one prompt. In 30 seconds, an AI writes hundreds of lines of code and builds a website.

Teacher: open gemini.google.com, press + then Canvas, and paste 
Create a colorful webpage for "Evergreen TV – Sports Desk", the sports corner of our school news platform, made by 12-year-old students.
Include: a big title with a TV logo, a scoreboard for 3 school football matches, a "Player of the Week" card, and a button that shows a random fun sports fact.
Use bright colors and big fonts.

Then ask the class: how many lines of code did the AI write? Open the code view and scroll.

02

You are the director

5 minutes · the lesson

The AI is the programmer. You are the director. It builds exactly what you describe, and guesses the rest.

Weak prompt

"Make a sports website."

The AI has to guess everything: which sport, which teams, what goes on the page, what it looks like.

Director's prompt

"Make 'Evergreen TV – Sports Desk' with a scoreboard for 3 school matches, a Player of the Week card and a fun-fact button. Bright colors, big fonts."

The AI knows what to build.

A director's prompt has 4 parts:

1. NameWhat is your corner called?
2. What's on it3 things on the page
3. LookColors, fonts, mood
4. ActionWhat happens when you click

    05

    Show your corner

    5 minutes · 3 students present

    Three students show their corner. The class checks it with these questions.

    Does it work?

    Click every button. Does each one do something?

    Is it clear?

    Can a visitor tell in 5 seconds what this corner is about?

    Is it safe?

    First names only, no photos of people, nothing private.

    Remember

    You wrote zero lines of code, but every idea on the page is yours. Next week your corner gets a character who talks to visitors.
