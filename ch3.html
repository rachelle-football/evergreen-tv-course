What is a prompt?

5 minutes · the words you give the AI

A prompt is the instruction you give AI to help turn your idea into a result. The AI does what you write, not what you think.

💡INPUTYour idea
→
💬PROMPTYour instructions
→
✨OUTPUTAI's result
🔁 Not good? Improve your prompt and try again

🔁 Not good? Improve your prompt and try again.

⚠️ Careful

A good prompt gives a much better result, but even with a perfect prompt, AI can still make mistakes. Always check what it gives you.

Try it: tap each short prompt. What does the AI have to guess?

Remember

If you don't say it, the AI guesses. A prompt engineer leaves nothing to guess.

Same AI, different prompts. Look at what changes.

✅ Strong prompt

What changed? Did we change the AI, or did we change our instructions?

Now with words. Same topic: space. Look at the two prompts, and at the two answers.

❌ Prompt 1 · weak

“Tell me about space.”

The AI's answer
Space is the vast, seemingly infinite expanse that exists beyond Earth's atmosphere. It contains a wide range of celestial bodies, including stars, planets, moons, asteroids, comets and galaxies, as well as phenomena such as nebulae, black holes and cosmic background radiation. The scientific study of space, known as astronomy and astrophysics, examines the origins, evolution and composition of the universe, from the Big Bang approximately 13.8 billion years ago…

😵 Too long, hard words, about everything. (…and 600 more words)

✅ Prompt 2 · strong

The AI's answer

Hi Grade 7! ☀️ The solar system is the Sun and everything that travels around it. Here are 5 amazing facts:

  1. About 1.3 million Earths could fit inside the Sun.
  2. There are 8 planets: 4 rocky ones close to the Sun, and 4 giants made of gas and ice.
  3. Jupiter is the biggest planet. Its Great Red Spot is a storm bigger than Earth!
  4. A day on Venus is longer than its whole year.
  5. Sunlight takes about 8 minutes to reach us.

🤩 Short, clear, made for you.
