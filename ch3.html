✅ Prompt 2 · strong

The AI's answer

Hi Grade 7! ☀️ The solar system is the Sun and everything that travels around it. Here are 5 amazing facts:

  1. About 1.3 million Earths could fit inside the Sun.
  2. There are 8 planets: 4 rocky ones close to the Sun, and 4 giants made of gas and ice.
  3. Jupiter is the biggest planet. Its Great Red Spot is a storm bigger than Earth!
  4. A day on Venus is longer than its whole year.
  5. Sunlight takes about 8 minutes to reach us.

🤩 Short, clear, made for you.

03

The 4 parts of a strong prompt

7 minutes · the prompt engineer's recipe

Every strong prompt has 4 parts. Learn them, and the AI works for you.

🎭
1 · Role

Who should the AI be?

You are a football coach.
🎯
2 · Task

What should it do? Be clear: one exact job.

Give me training exercises to improve my speed.
📚
3 · Context

Who is it for? Why? What should it know?

I am 12, I play left wing, and I train twice a week.
📋
4 · Output

How should the answer look? A list, a table, how long?

Give me 5 exercises, in a short list, in simple English.
🔁
And the secret 5th step: improve!

Not happy with the answer? Don't start again. Ask for a change: Make it shorter. Add an example. Explain it like I'm 10.
