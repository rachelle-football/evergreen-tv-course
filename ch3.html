Teacher: now ask Gemini the same question

Gemini answers almost anything. Nobody wrote its answers: it learned from a huge amount of text and predicts the next words. But it only knows what you tell it about YOUR project. That is why the prompt matters.

02

The prompt recipe

8 minutes · the lesson

Weak prompt

"Design a future classroom."

Gemini does not know your school, your age or your country. So it gives a boring, general answer that could be anywhere.

Recipe prompt

Role + Task + Context + Format

Tell it who to be, what to do, the facts it can't know, and how the answer should look. Now the answer is about YOUR project.

Build your recipe in 4 clicks: one choice in each box. This is the start of YOUR mission prompt: in part 03 you add the details and send it to Gemini.
