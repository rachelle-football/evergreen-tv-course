01

Your own website

Period 1 · 8 minutes · the lesson

Today you are the web designer. You build a real website, with its own link, that you can send to your family.

❌ A weak prompt

"Make a website about me."

The AI does not know you. It invents a boring page that could be about anyone.

✅ A strong prompt

"A website about my team Real Madrid, in Stadium Night style, white and gold, with my player Vinícius and 3 fun facts I know."

Every detail is yours, so the website is yours.

A website prompt has the same 4 parts you know:

🎭 Role

Who builds it? "You are a web designer who builds fun websites for kids."

🎯 Task

About what, and for who? "A website about my dream job, for my class and my family."

📚 Context

Your real details: names, why you love it, facts you know. The most important part: what you do not write, the AI invents.

📋 Format

How it looks: the title, the style, the colours, and one fun button.

In part 03, every choice you make writes one line of your website's prompt, and you see your website change at once.
