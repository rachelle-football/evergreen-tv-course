You are the director

Period 1 · 6 minutes · teacher demo, then the lesson

Watch: the teacher types one prompt in Gemini Canvas. In 30 seconds, the AI writes hundreds of lines of code and builds a web page.

Teacher: open gemini.google.com, press + then Canvas, and paste 
Create a colourful one-page website called "School 2040: The Sky Playground", made by a 12-year-old student.
Include: a big title, 3 cards with big ideas (a rooftop garden, a climbing wall, a rain-water fountain), and a button that shows a random fun fact about playgrounds.
Bright colours, big fonts, works on a phone.
Weak prompt

"Make a website about water."

The AI guesses everything: what goes on it, the colours, the text. It is not YOUR page.

Director's prompt

Name + what's on it + look + action

You give it your design, your story and your files. The AI is the programmer. You are the director.
