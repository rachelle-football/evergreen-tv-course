Can AI teach itself?

5 minutes · a true story from 2017

An AI called AlphaZero was given only the rules of chess. No tips, no famous games. Then it played against itself.

Practice time0 h
Its teacheritself
After about 9 hours

It played the best chess program in the world, 100 games: 28 wins, 0 losses, 72 draws.

But who is the boss?

People chose the goal (win at chess), built the AI and decided what it is used for. AI can get better at a task by itself. People decide which task.
