What is AI?

7 minutes · rules vs. examples

Challenge: write a rule a computer could follow to know if an animal is a cat.

Pick a rule. Let's see if it works.

Choose a rule above.

So what is AI?

AI is a computer that learns from examples, instead of following rules that a person wrote.

Show it thousands of photos labelled "cat" and "not a cat", and it finds the patterns by itself. Nobody writes the rule.

Example numbers, to show the idea. ChatGPT works this way: it picks a likely next word, again and again.

05

Can AI teach itself?

5 minutes · a true story from 2017

An AI called AlphaZero was given only the rules of chess. No tips, no famous games. Then it played against itself.

Practice time0 h
Its teacheritself
After about 9 hours

It played the best chess program in the world, 100 games: 28 wins, 0 losses, 72 draws.

But who is the boss?

People chose the goal (win at chess), built the AI and decided what it is used for. AI can get better at a task by itself. People decide which task.

Engineer

Chef

Teacher

The people who know how to use AI will lead. You can start today.
