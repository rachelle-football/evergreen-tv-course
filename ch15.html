Example · student prototype

Example made up for this lesson, with fictional data. Click the buttons.

4 rules of a good app
  1. One main job per screen. The user knows at once what it is for.
  2. Big, clear buttons with words that say what they do: “I lost something”, not “Go”.
  3. Simple words and a short title.
  4. Feedback: when you click, something happens or a message appears.

Build with AI

Period 1 · 20 minutes · Gemini is your coding assistant

A web app is made of 3 languages. You don't learn them by heart: you learn what each one does.

🧱 HTMLStructure: the title, the buttons, the lists. Like the walls and rooms of a house.
🎨 CSSDesign: colours, sizes, spaces. Like the paint and furniture.
⚡ JavaScriptInteraction: what happens when you click. Like the electricity.
🤖 AIYour coding assistant. It writes code fast, but YOU decide and YOU check.
Step 1 · Ask Gemini to build your app

↗ Open Gemini

In Gemini: paste (Ctrl+V), press Enter, then click Copy code at the top of the code box.

🔒 Your app runs in a safe, closed box: it can't see your account, your saved work or other pages.

Step 3 · Did it work?
🐞 Bug report
