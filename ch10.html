02

Why does AI get things wrong?

10 minutes · learn

AI answers can contain errors, missing information or invented details. Important information must be checked.

Reason 1🎲 It guesses the most likely word

It does not look things up. It writes the word that comes most often.

Example

“The capital of Australia is Sydney.”

Sydney is the city people write about most, so the AI guesses it. The real capital is Canberra.
Reason 2📚 It learned from old or wrong texts

People wrote its examples. Some were wrong, some are old.

Example

“There are 9 planets in our solar system.”

Old books said 9. Since 2006, Pluto is a dwarf planet: there are 8.
Reason 3😎 It always sounds sure

A wrong answer sounds exactly as confident as a right one.

Example

“Of course! The Great Wall of China is easy to see from space.”

Sounds sure, but false: astronauts say you can't see it with your eyes from space.
