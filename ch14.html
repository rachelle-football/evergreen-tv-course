Remember

02

Think like an innovator

10 minutes · learn

1 · The user

Who has the problem?

Grade 7 students? Teachers? The cleaning staff?

2 · The need

What do they need?

Not “an app”: what do they need to DO? e.g. find the homework fast.

3 · How might we…?

Turn it into a question

“How might we help students find their homework instructions?”

4 · Success

How will we know it works?

Something you can see or count: “students find it in under 1 minute”.

Key words. Tap each one.
