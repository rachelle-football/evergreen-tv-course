How AI video works, and how to catch it

Period 1 · 7 minutes · the lesson

How it works

A video is about 25 pictures every second. An AI video model makes all of those pictures from your words, and each one has to match the one before. That is hard, so it slips.

Teacher: show one AI-made clip you prepared on your own account, then one real clip. Ask: which is which, and how can you tell?

Signs of an AI video
  • Hands with too many or melting fingers
  • Writing that turns into scribbles
  • Things that appear or vanish between frames
  • Lips that do not match the words
  • Skin too smooth, light that makes no sense

Before you believe or share anything, do the 4 S check:

SourceWho made it? Do you trust them?
SearchCan you find it on 2 trusted sites?
SignsAny of the AI signs above?
Slow downVery angry or scared? Wait before sharing.
