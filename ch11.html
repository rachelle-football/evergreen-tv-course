01

Spot the AI's choices

Period 1 · 5 minutes · warm-up

We asked an AI for a picture. It gave us four in ten seconds. Look closely: what did it decide by itself?

Our prompt

"Bright cartoon TV news studio for a school sports show, a big screen showing a football, a desk with a microphone, colorful, fun, for kids"

Made with Adobe Express by our teacher, 10 November 2026.

Choice 1 · the ball

We wrote “football”. In Lebanon that means soccer, but in America it means a game with an oval ball. Our word was unclear, so the AI picked one meaning.

Choice 2 · the presenter

We never asked for a person. The AI added a presenter in 3 of the 4 pictures, and every time it chose a boy. Never a girl.

Remember

AI sometimes makes assumptions when our instructions are unclear. The word “football” can have different meanings. AI may also add details that we never asked for. When it makes the same choice every time (a boy, every time), that can be unfair: we call it bias. Always check what the AI decided.

02

How AI generates images

Period 1 · 6 minutes · the lesson

Watch: from noise to picture
Step 0 of 30
  1. It studied millions of pictures with captions, like “a red apple on a table”.
  2. It starts a new picture as random dots, like TV snow.
  3. Step by step, it cleans the dots into a picture that matches your words.
  4. Where your words are unclear, it guesses. That is why it chose the oval ball and a boy.

Many image AI tools work like this, but not all of them.

Your job today

You already know how to write a strong prompt (Chapter 4). Today you learn the words of pictures, voices and videos, and you make your own commercial.

✓ Deliverable 2 · My advertising voice-over

A voice is not only words. It has emotion, speed and tone. In Period 3 you turn your script into a real voice file for your video.

⚠️ Voices can be copied

AI can copy a real person's voice from a short recording, and make it “say” anything. Never copy or use someone's voice without their permission. Use only computer voices in your work.
