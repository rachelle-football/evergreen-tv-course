02

AI for voice, audio and video

10 minutes · learn

🔊 Text-to-speech

Text → voice

You type words, the computer reads them aloud. Used in GPS, audiobooks, and to help people who can't read a screen.

📝 Speech-to-text

Voice → text

You speak, the computer writes. Used for subtitles, voice messages and dictation on your phone.

🎬 Video planning

Message → storyboard

Every good video starts with a clear message and a plan of its scenes, before anyone films anything.

Try text-to-speech

Change the speed and tone. Same words, different feeling. Nothing is recorded: the voice comes from your computer.

The idea

Before making a video, you plan it on paper: a storyboard. For each scene you write 2 things: 👁 what we see and 🎙 what the narrator says. 4 short scenes, about 70 words: that's 30 seconds. In your mission, you plan your own.
