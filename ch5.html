Spot the AI's choices

5 minutes · warm-up

We asked an AI for a picture. It gave us four in ten seconds. Look closely: what did it decide by itself?

Our prompt

"Bright cartoon TV news studio for a school sports show, a big screen showing a football, a desk with a microphone, colorful, fun, for kids"

Made with Adobe Express by our teacher, 10 November 2026.

Choice 1 · the ball

The ball is oval: an American football. Most of the pictures it learned from come from America, where "football" means that game.

Choice 2 · the presenter

We never asked for a person. It added one anyway, and chose a boy, four times out of four.

02

How an AI makes a picture

7 minutes · the lesson

Watch: from noise to picture
Step 0 of 30
  1. It studied millions of pictures with captions, like "a red apple on a table".
  2. It starts every new picture as random dots, like TV snow.
  3. Step by step, it cleans the dots into something that matches your words.
  4. Where your words are vague, it guesses from what it saw most often. That is why it drew an American football and a boy.

So your prompt must leave less to guess. A strong picture prompt has 5 ingredients:

SubjectWhat is in it: "a round black and white soccer ball"
SettingWhere: "a classroom full of plants"
StyleCartoon, 3D, watercolour, pixel art
LightBright, sunset, neon, night
CameraClose-up, wide shot, from above
